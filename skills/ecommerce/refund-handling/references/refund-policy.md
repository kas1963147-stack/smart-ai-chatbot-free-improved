# Refund Policy

This document contains the detailed refund policy for the store.

## Standard Return Policy

### Eligibility
- Items must be unused and in original packaging
- Customer must have proof of purchase (order ID or receipt)
- Return request must be initiated within the eligible window

### Timeframes
| Period | Refund Type | Conditions |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| 0-14 days | Full refund | No questions asked |
| 15-30 days | Full refund | Item must be unused |
| 31-60 days | Store credit | 15% restocking fee may apply |
| 60+ days | No refund | Warranty claims only |

## Non-Returnable Items
- Personalized or custom items
- Perishable goods
- Intimate items (underwear, swimwear)
- Gift cards
- Downloaded digital products

## Damaged or Defective Items
- Report within 48 hours of delivery
- Photo evidence required
- Full replacement or refund offered
- No return shipping required

## Sale Items
- Final sale items (marked): No returns
- Discounted items: Store credit only
- BOGO promotions: Return both or neither

## Processing Times
- Refund approval: 1-2 business days
- Credit card refund: 5-10 business days
- Store credit: Immediate
- Bank transfer: 3-5 business days

## Shipping Costs
- Defective items: We cover return shipping
- Buyer's remorse: Customer pays return shipping
- Wrong item sent: We cover shipping both ways

## Contact for Exceptions
For special circumstances, escalate to customer service manager.
